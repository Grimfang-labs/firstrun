# Code of conduct

Participants must treat others respectfully, welcome good-faith questions, and keep feedback focused on the work. Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, and disclosure of another person's private information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, close discussions, or restrict participation proportionately. They should explain decisions where doing so does not expose private information. A person involved in a complaint should not decide its outcome alone when another organization maintainer is available.

For non-sensitive moderation requests, contact a maintainer in the relevant issue. For sensitive reports, use a private contact channel listed on an organization owner's profile and ask for a reporting channel before sending personal details. Do not post sensitive allegations publicly. A dedicated reporting contact should be established before broad community outreach.
